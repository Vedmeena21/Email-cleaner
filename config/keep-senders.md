# Keep senders

Mail from these senders is never moved or labelled by any rule, on any account.

Formats:
- `person@example.com`: that exact address
- `@example.com` or `example.com`: anyone at example.com or its subdomains
- `noreply@*`: wildcards are allowed in the address part

Add one bullet per sender under "Keep" below. Anything after `#` is a comment.
